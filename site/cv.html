Research interests

  • Fluid-structure interaction: Flow-induced vibration, hydroelasticity and aeroelasticity, fluid-thermal-structure interaction, and flexible or morphing structures.
  • Flow-induced noise: Aeroacoustics and hydroacoustics, cavitation-induced noise, and coupled fluid-structure-acoustic mechanisms in wind turbines and marine propulsion.
  • High-fidelity and data-driven modeling: CFD/FSI multiphysics, structural and fluid reduced-order models, graph neural networks, and machine-learning-based closure models for rapid prediction.
  • Design, optimization and digital twins: Bio-inspired and internal topology design, inverse optimization, adaptive monitoring, and feedback control.

Software / packages developed

  • OFCCX (3D parallel high-fidelity fluid-structure interaction framework for aerospace and energy systems)

    Developed a parallel FSI framework for high-Reynolds-number flows with internal structural topology, structural nonlinearity, and hyperelasticity. The solver supports high-fidelity analysis of strongly coupled aerospace and marine FSI problems and provides a physics-based foundation for design optimization and multiphysics modeling.

  • UFVROM (Reduced-order modeling framework for aeroelasticity and flow-induced vibration)

    Developed a system-identification-based reduced-order model for rapid prediction and stability analysis of aeroelastic/FIV systems. A submodal superposition approach for three-dimensional non-uniform inflow achieved up to 2000× acceleration while maintaining accuracy, enabling real-time monitoring and digital-twin applications. Modal diagnostics captured beating, Hopf bifurcation, and spatial instability.

  • FSIML (Graph neural network framework for fluid-structure interaction prediction)

    Combined graph neural networks with proper orthogonal decomposition to address mesh adaptation and boundary-treatment limitations of conventional CNNs in FSI. The framework generalizes across flexible/morphing wings, flow-induced vibration, and rotating dynamics, providing fast surrogates for aerospace prediction, inverse design, and digital-twin control.
