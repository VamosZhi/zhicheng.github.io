About my work

I connect the physics of fluid-structure interaction with rapid prediction and design.

At UC Berkeley, I work with Grace X. Gu on aerospace topology design and graph neural networks for coupled fluid–structure systems. Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of British Columbia with Rajeev Jaiman. My doctoral research at Waterloo and Duke, supervised by Fue-Sang Lien and Earl H. Dowell, combined computational modeling with wind-tunnel experiments.

Research in focus

Coupled physics.
Engineering insight.

From the onset of structural instability to the radiation of sound, I investigate the mechanisms that connect flow, motion, and performance.

01 — fluid-structure interaction

Small geometric changes.
Different stability.

My research examines how fore–aft tapering changes the onset of galloping. Numerical simulations and experiments show that even a one-degree change in cross-sectional angle can alter the instability response in the configurations studied.

Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 2023
