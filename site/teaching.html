Teaching & mentorship

Intuition. Theory. Practice.

I help students connect physical insight to mathematical models and hands-on computation, with clear explanations and constructive feedback.

Teaching philosophy

Begin with a physical question.
Build the tools to answer it.

Learning through engineering problems

I begin with a concrete engineering problem, introduce the physical principles and mathematical tools behind it, and reinforce the concepts through discussion, coding, or hands-on activities. My goal is to help students develop both technical fluency and the confidence to reason through unfamiliar problems.

Supporting different starting points

I value an inclusive learning environment in which students can ask questions and contribute their perspectives. My approach combines visual explanations, collaborative learning, and project presentations. I aim to provide multiple ways to seek help, including office hours, discussion forums, and anonymous feedback.

Research-informed mentoring

I have assisted in supervising two master’s students on thesis projects and supported new researchers with problem formulation, experiments, and scientific writing. I plan to incorporate open-ended research projects into advanced courses and help students connect classroom tools to real engineering questions.
